FIELD SOLUTION SPECIFICATION

GAS SERVICE OPERATIONS

Scope of Field Operations

Comprehensive gas distribution field services with operator-qualified technicians adhering strictly to local, state, and DOT 49 CFR Part 192 compliance standards.

✓ Veriforce OQ Certified ✓ GPS Telemetry Dispatched ✓ Full Digital Auditing
Key Capabilities & Field Controls
  • Trained and evaluated under rigorous Operator Qualification (OQ) programs.
  • Real-time work order submission with timestamped photographic proof of completion.
  • Equipped with calibrated combustible gas indicators (CGI) and digital pressure gauges.
  • Adherence to strict OSHA PPE standards and utility partner operating manuals.
VERIFORCE COMPLIANCE

OUR SAFETY & REGULATORY STANDARDS

At United Gas Solutions, safety is embedded in every field procedure, briefing, and job site validation.

Veriforce Operator Qualification (OQ)

100% of field technicians maintain current Veriforce OQ credentials covering covered tasks under DOT 192 rules.

Daily Job Safety Analysis (JSA)

Every work order begins with an on-site digital safety assessment and hazard mitigation verification.

Zero Incident Protocol

Continuous safety auditing, stop-work authority empowerment for all personnel, and weekly training drills.

Calibrated Equipment Assurance

Combustible gas detectors, pressure manifolds, and electrofusion equipment calibrated and certified regularly.
