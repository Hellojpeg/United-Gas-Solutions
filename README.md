## ⚡ Core Field Capabilities

1. **Gas Service Operations** — Distribution field services, leak response, valve maintenance, DOT 49 CFR Part 192 compliance.
2. **Meter Services & Replacements** — Residential & commercial gas meter sets, cyclic exchanges, AMR/AMI retrofits.
3. **Turn-Ons, Relights & Pressure Testing** — High/low pressure test validation, appliance relighting, manifold lock-outs.
4. **Disconnects & Emergency Shut-Offs** — Authorized non-pay shutoffs, hazard mitigation, safety tag-outs.
5. **Meter Renewals & Atmospheric Corrosion** — Riser remediation, grit blasting, high-spec epoxy coating, compliance documentation.
6. **Field Inspections & Compliance Surveys** — Walking leak surveys, cathodic protection (CP) reads, Veriforce-audited logs.
7. **Emergency Workforce & Storm Support** — 24/7 rapid deployment across the East Coast during natural disasters and pipeline events.
8. **Managed Operations & Turnkey Dispatch** — Real-time telemetry, dedicated supervisor crews, integrated utility portal syncing.
